# Screenplay Finder Reader — Privacy Policy

> Draft for review — updated 29 October 2026. Effective date will be set on publication.

Screenplay Finder Reader is provided by Christopher Edward Green, trading as
Media Grid Software Development Labs, ABN 98 783 256 814, Australia. Contact:
support@mgsdl.com.

## Information stored on your device

The Reader stores imported catalogues, searches, favourites, private tags,
shortlists, notes, settings and correction proposals in local application storage.
It does not require an account. The Reader does not send your catalogue contents,
searches or personal collections to us automatically, and does not include
advertising, tracking or analytics services.

Ordinary catalogue searching and reading included material work offline.
The Reader does not provide cloud synchronisation or automatically upload your
screenplays or collections.

## Files you choose to import, export or share

You control catalogue imports, personal-data backups, metadata-list exports and
correction-feedback exports. A personal backup can contain private notes and
collections. Correction-feedback exports contain the selected proposals and
associated catalogue identities and published/proposed values; they exclude
private collection notes and screenplay text.

Printed or shared metadata lists exclude your private notes by default, but can
include them if you select that option. Review exports before sharing: information
you enter into notes or correction fields may itself be personal or sensitive.

Choosing a cloud file provider or sharing service may send your chosen files to
that provider or recipient. Their privacy practices apply. System/device backups
may also include application data, depending on your device settings.

## TestFlight testing

When you use the TestFlight beta, Apple processes testing information and makes
information available to us, including build usage, crashes, tester invitation
status and, depending on invitation method, your name and email address. Crash
and usage information is collected automatically by Apple during TestFlight
testing. Feedback
may include screenshots, device/OS details and contact information. We use this
information to investigate problems, respond to testers and improve the Reader.
Please avoid including sensitive information or copyrighted screenplay text in
screenshots and feedback unless necessary and appropriate to share.

See [Apple's TestFlight privacy information](https://images.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/)
for details of Apple's processing and retention.

## Support and voluntary feedback

If you contact us or send a feedback package, we receive the information you
choose to provide, such as your email address, message and attachments. We use it
to provide support, review corrections and improve the software. Email and hosting
providers process correspondence and website requests to deliver those services;
processing may occur outside Australia. Our support email is received using
Apple iCloud Mail. Apple processes email to provide that service; see
[Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).
We do not sell your personal information or use support correspondence for
advertising.

## Website hosting

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for
security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See
[GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages)
and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for information about its processing. These website requests are separate from
local use of the Reader app.

The reviewed website uses local assets and does not add advertising, analytics,
tracking scripts, contact forms or embedded third-party media. Links to other
websites are governed by those websites' privacy practices when you follow them.
Website hosting and email services may process information outside Australia.


## Retention and deletion

Local Reader data remains until you remove it using the app or operating system.
Removing a catalogue may retain its personal data if you choose that option.
Copies in exported files, cloud storage and device backups must be managed there
separately.

We normally delete identifiable support
correspondence and downloaded tester feedback within 12 months after the issue is
closed, unless needed for an ongoing request or legal obligation. We may retain
technical issue records after removing personal information and attachments.

Contact us to request access to or correction/deletion of information you have
sent us, or to raise a privacy concern. We cannot retrieve or delete local-only
Reader data remotely. Apple manages information held in its services under its
own policies.

## Changes

We will update this page when these practices change and show the effective date
above. Material changes affecting a beta will also be explained to testers.
