# Reader support

Contact support@mgsdl.com for help with Screenplay Finder Reader.
For beta feedback you can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Please tell us your device model, operating-system version, Reader build, what
you were doing and what happened. Do not send a complete screenplay or personal
backup unless we have discussed what is needed. Crop sensitive information from
screenshots.

### Getting started

Open the included collection and choose a piece to see its details. Use Read
when an approved reading copy is included. Use filters to narrow the results;
role profiles can rank preferred matches or require a match, depending on the
strict-role setting. Save favourites and shortlists under My collections.

### Importing catalogues

Use the catalogue import control and select the .sfcatalog file supplied by your
curator. The Reader validates the package before installation. A metadata-only
catalogue does not contain the screenplay. Obtain source material lawfully and
follow the permissions provided with each collection.

### Protecting your selections

Use the catalogue's personal backup export before replacing a device or restoring
data. Keep the matching catalogue package as well as the backup. Restoring a
backup replaces that catalogue's current personal data; export it first if you
want to preserve it. The Reader does not automatically synchronise devices.

### Text size and accessibility

The iPhone/iPad Reader follows system text-size settings and also provides an
in-app larger-text option. VoiceOver is supported. If a control is difficult to
use, please report the screen, device and text-size/accessibility settings.
