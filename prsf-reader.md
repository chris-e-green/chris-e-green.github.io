# Screenplay Finder Reader

Find material for auditions, acting classes and rehearsal.

Search prepared screenplay catalogues for monologues, two-person scenes and
larger scenes. Narrow your search by length, roles, casting profiles, tone and
other supplied catalogue information. Save favourites and organise selections
into private shortlists.

The Reader works locally on iPhone and iPad. Where a catalogue includes an
approved reading copy, you can read the material in the app. Other catalogues
provide information to help you locate it in a separately obtained lawful copy.
Availability of source text depends on the individual catalogue's permissions.

The current TestFlight beta includes selections from Oscar Wilde's The Importance
of Being Earnest, with screenplay adaptation by Christopher E. Green. Credits and
permissions are available in the app. Metadata and advisory content notes can be
incomplete; review material yourself before choosing it for a class or audition.

Beta access is by invitation; contact [support@mgsdl.com](mailto:support@mgsdl.com)
for enquiries. There is no charge for this beta. Your searches and
collections stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.
